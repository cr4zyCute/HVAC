COMMON WARNING SIGNS

Is Your Air Conditioner Showing These Symptoms?

Recognizing system distress early prevents total compressor burnout and costly emergency replacement.

!

AC Blowing Warm Air

Caused by low refrigerant charge, faulty compressor relays, or a blown dual capacitor.

!

Strange Grinding or Buzzing

Worn blower motor bearings, loose fan blades, or failing electrical contactor coils.

!

Weak Airflow from Vents

Restricted air filters, failing blower motors, or ductwork disconnections inside the attic.

!

Frequent System Cycling (Short Cycling)

Unit cycles on and off every few minutes without reaching thermostat setpoint, rapidly spiking power bills and stressing internal electrical relays.

!

Water Leaking Around Indoor Unit

Clogged condensate drain lines or cracked drain pan overflowing into drywall, creating structural water damage risk. Shut unit off immediately.
